# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-13

First public release.

- Three modes: auto, manual, curve
- Draggable multi-point fan curve, with saved presets
- Live temperature sidebar, 12 named sensors
- Light and dark themes, Chinese and English UI
- Menu-bar app, single instance, helper installs itself
- Built-in diagnose and verify, plus the `fanctl` CLI
- App icon
